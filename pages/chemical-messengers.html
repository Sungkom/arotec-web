WHAT ARE NEUROTRANSMITTERS?

THE CHEMICAL MESSENGERS OF THE BRAIN

Neurotransmitters are chemical messengers released by neurons to transmit signals across synapses.

  • Fast signaling
  • Local / synaptic communication
  • Brain and nervous system regulation
  • Influence on mood, arousal, cognition, and movement

EXAMPLES

  • Serotonin
  • Dopamine
  • GABA
  • Glutamate
  • Norepinephrine
02

WHAT ARE HORMONES?

THE BODY’S SYSTEMIC CHEMICAL SIGNALS

Hormones are chemical messengers released by endocrine glands and tissues and carried through the bloodstream to regulate whole-body functions.

  • Slower but broader signaling
  • Systemic / body-wide communication
  • Endocrine and metabolic regulation
  • Influence on circadian timing, stress, growth, and energy balance

EXAMPLES

  • Melatonin
  • Cortisol
  • Growth Hormone
  • Insulin
  • Leptin & Ghrelin
