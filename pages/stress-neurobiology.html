The four stages of ‘stress’

(A logical progression simplified)

Adapted from Selye’s General Adaptation Syndrome (GAS), simplified for educational purposes — not a clinical diagnostic model.

Homeostasis & adaptive response Distress predominance

1. Normal

(Homeostasis)

  • Stable internal environment
  • Normal stress response
  • Recovery capacity intact

2. Acute phase

(Resistance)

  • Stress response activated
  • Adaptive mechanisms mobilized
  • Temporary increase
    in stress hormones

3. Compensatory phase

(Attempted Adaptation)

  • Continued stress exposure
  • Physiological wear and tear
  • Adaptation becoming insufficient
  • Energy resources declining

4. Exhaustion phase

(Failed Adaptation)

  • System resources depleted
  • Dysregulation of stress systems
  • Reduced resilience
  • Increased risk of dysfunction
    and disease

Note: individual responses vary —
chronic stress may lead to persistently
elevated or low cortisol.
