Can you feel
a flavor?

Flavor is more than taste.
It is a full-body sensation.

Some flavors cool. Some warm.
Some brighten, soften, sparkle or satisfy.

Sensation makes flavor come alive and turns consumption into experience.

  • Cool

    Menthol / Eucalyptus

    Cooling sensation that refreshes.

  • Hot

    Chili / Ginger

    Warming stimulation.

  • Sharp

    Vinegar / Green Apple

    Bright tingling.

  • Creamy

    Milk / Vanilla

    Smooth and rounded.

  • Fizzy

    Carbonation

    Lively and exciting.

  • Chewy

    Boba / Jelly

    Playful and satisfying.

This is
synesthesia.

When senses connect,
experience changes.

Cross-sensory
integration

The brain weaves together visual, olfactory, tactile, auditory, and emotional cues into a unified perception—shaping flavor in ways greater than the sum of its parts.

Neural pathways don’t work in isolation—they communicate. That’s where flavor becomes experience.
