SECTION 2 / EXPOSOME FACTORS

EXPOSOME FACTORS

How external and internal exposures shape skin health.

Environmental and lifestyle exposures influence skin biology, impacting barrier function, inflammation,
and long-term beauty, health, and wellbeing.

  1. 1

    AIR POLLUTION
    & CHEMICALS

    Particulate matter and chemical exposures contribute to oxidative stress, inflammation, and barrier damage.

  2. 2

    UV / BLUE LIGHT
    & CLIMATE

    UV radiation, blue light, temperature extremes, and humidity disrupt skin defense and accelerate aging.

  3. 3

    STRESS &
    PSYCHOSOCIAL LOAD

    Chronic stress and emotional strain dysregulate skin responses and worsen inflammatory pathways.

  4. 4

    POOR
    SLEEP

    Insufficient or poor-quality sleep impairs repair, weakens barrier function, and dulls skin appearance.

  5. 5

    UNHEALTHY DIET &
    LIFESTYLE IMBALANCE

    Poor nutrition, inactivity, smoking, and excess alcohol disrupt skin health and accelerate aging.

  6. 6

    MICROBIOME &
    METABOLIC FACTORS

    Microbiome imbalance and metabolic dysfunction affect immunity, inflammation, and barrier integrity.

KEY MESSAGE

Reducing harmful exposures helps protect skin barrier function
and long-term beauty, health, and wellbeing.
