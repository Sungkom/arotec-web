Cookie and browser-storage policy

Draft effective date: 5 October 2026

This draft explains browser storage used by the new localized Arotec pilot pages. Remembering your language is optional and starts off. Please review the owner details and provider information listed below before publication.

Pages covered by this draft

This policy covers the new homepage, Neuro-Skin Science, Co-Creation, Get In Touch and this policy page in Thai, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese: 25 localized pages, plus the neutral language chooser, making 26 pilot pages in total.

Other existing pages, member services and administration pages are outside this pilot policy. Older language-switching code uses the localStorage key as-site-language without a built-in expiry. That code is omitted from the new pilot pages; a previously stored legacy key may remain in your browser until you clear it.

Optional remembered language

The pilot uses one first-party localStorage key, arotec-storage-preferences-v1, for an explicit language-storage choice. It does not need this record to display a page or follow a language link. The record contains version 1, whether remembering is enabled, a remembered locale only when enabled, and savedAt and expiresAt timestamps. It does not contain your name, contact-form message, password or payment-card details.

A record is first written when you explicitly save a choice or choose to use the pages without remembering. An off-choice record retains no remembered language and helps the pages respect your decision. While remembering is enabled, selecting another language with an ordinary language-link click updates the remembered locale and starts a new validity period of up to 180 days. A page URL that names a language always takes priority. Only the language-selection landing page may use a valid remembered language to choose a destination.

Each saved record is valid for at most 180 days from the latest saved choice or opted-in language-link update. localStorage has no automatic browser expiry: the pilot ignores and removes an invalid or expired record when it next checks the preference. Optional remembering then defaults off.

Change or clear your choice

Use Storage settings to reopen the controls. The Remember this language for 180 days checkbox is off by default. Save preferences records your explicit selection; Use without remembering records an off choice. Opening, scrolling or closing the notice does not turn remembering on.

Clear remembered language records remembering as off with no saved locale, using the same key. If saving the off choice fails, the site attempts to remove its own preference key and verify that remembering is off. If removal cannot be confirmed because storage is inaccessible, remembering is off for the current page, but the previous saved preference may return after a reload or later visit. The site cannot promise deletion or a persistent off choice in that situation. Otherwise, future visits and reloads respect the valid saved choice or verified removal. The pages and language links still work when storage is blocked or unavailable.

You can also remove site storage using your browser settings. These controls manage this pilot’s own preference record; they cannot promise to remove storage set by other sites or inaccessible third parties.

Fonts and hosting requests

The pilot does not enable optional analytics, marketing trackers or embedded media. Some fonts are provided by local files; where Google Fonts is used, loading a page requests font resources from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. The provider may receive the IP address and request information needed to serve those resources. A font request is different from this optional language-storage record.

GitHub Pages hosts the current pilot. Hosting and font providers may process or log requests under their own terms. Their exact logging, retention and any server-side cookie behavior require owner review and runtime verification; this draft does not claim that no cookies exist anywhere on the wider site.

Contact and review status

The existing website publishes arotec@arotec-group.com as a contact email. The owner must confirm that it is the correct privacy contact, and supply the legal entity name and address before this draft is finalized. Email delivery has not been tested.

This is a factual implementation draft, not a guarantee of legal compliance. The owner must review applicable jurisdictions, lawful bases, provider arrangements and any additional storage found before publication.
