# Human Aging Biomedical Icon System

- 21 final PNG assets with true RGBA transparency.
- Standard icons: 512 × 512 px.
- EBI exposome wheel: 1024 × 1024 px.
- Visual direction: premium biomedical line art, cyan/ice-blue molecular and neural forms, rose organ-system forms, crisp white highlights, subtle glass-enamel depth, and strong readability on the dark navy interface used by `human-longevity-aging.html`.
- Original files in `icons2` were not overwritten.

## Final generation prompts

### Molecular hallmarks and ADI

Create a precise 4 × 2 icon atlas containing exactly eight isolated biomedical icons: damaged DNA, telomere chromosome, epigenetic DNA, proteostasis network, biological cell, molecular network, brain, and older adult with cane. Use premium cyan/electric-blue/ice-white biomedical line art with glass-enamel depth, no text, no frames, and a uniform `#00ff00` background.

### Organ and nervous systems

Create a precise 3 × 2 icon atlas containing exactly six isolated anatomical icons: heart, lungs, liver, skeletal muscle fascicle, brain, and neuron. Use rose/coral highlights for organs and cyan/ice-blue for brain and neuron, with clean luminous outlines, no text, no frames, and a uniform `#00ff00` background.

### Energy, body composition, and homeostasis

Create a precise 4 × 2 icon atlas containing seven isolated biomedical icons: human body, mitochondrion, metabolic molecule, body-composition scan, three cells, homeostasis network, and older adult scan. Use premium cyan/electric-blue/ice-white scientific iconography, no text, no frames, and a uniform `#00ff00` background.

### EBI exposome wheel

Create one sophisticated circular exposome wheel with a central human figure and exactly eight surrounding exposure medallions connected by fine luminous lines. Use deep navy, cobalt, cyan, ice-white, and restrained violet accents, no text, and a uniform `#00ff00` background.

The green backgrounds were converted to soft-matte alpha transparency with edge despill before individual cropping, normalization, and export.
